AOIDOS 2.1
HYBRID LANGUAGE–DECISION INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM
Developed by Oblivion
PROJECT OVERVIEW
Aoidos 2.1 is an experimental hybrid artificial intelligence system designed to coordinate a Language Model (LLM) with an independent Decision Model within a unified operational architecture.
The project explores an approach that goes beyond conventional model connectivity.
Rather than treating different AI models as isolated services exchanging simple inputs and outputs, Aoidos aims to establish functional coordination between language intelligence and decision intelligence.
The system has been implemented and experimentally tested by the Oblivion development team.
It currently remains in a controlled laboratory development phase and is not publicly released.
CORE OBJECTIVE
The primary objective of Aoidos is to coordinate two distinct computational capabilities:
1. LANGUAGE INTELLIGENCE
Responsible for natural-language understanding, contextual interpretation, information processing, and response generation.
2. DECISION INTELLIGENCE
Responsible for decision-oriented evaluation, comparison of alternatives, prioritization, and structured selection processes.
Aoidos coordinates these capabilities within a shared workflow.
The participating models maintain their independent underlying architectures. The system-level integration does not require merging their neural network weights.
ARCHITECTURAL DIRECTION
Aoidos is designed around the following principles:
- Functional coordination instead of simple model connectivity.
- Separation of language and decision responsibilities.
- Structured interaction between independent components.
- Modular architecture and component flexibility.
- Experimental evaluation of integrated behavior.
- Extensibility toward additional models and computational capabilities.
The architecture focuses on making different intelligence components work together as parts of one operational system.
